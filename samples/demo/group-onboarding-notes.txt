Group onboarding notes: computational materials (DFT)

Our group predicts the structure and properties of oxide thin films with density functional theory (VASP).
Typical first project: relax the bulk crystal, check the lattice constant against XRD, then build a slab
and compute surface energies. Convergence tests (k-points, cutoff energy) must be done before anything else.
Common mistakes of newcomers: skipping convergence tests, comparing energies from different settings,
and trusting band gaps from plain GGA (they are usually too small; use hybrid functionals or DFT+U).
Collaboration: our experimental partners grow the films (PLD, sputtering) and measure XRD, SEM and XPS.
They want simulations that explain which defects form after annealing and how strain changes the structure.
