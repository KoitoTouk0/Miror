# 01 · Project brief

## The hackathon

- **Project:** "Explorer", a tool that helps people expand their knowledge.
- **Due:** Thu 22 Oct 2026, 19:00. **Submission:** a link plus a 4-minute video.
- **Source of intent:** `Intact_drive_undecorated.md`, the user's raw notes (never edit). `Revised_outline.md` was the Wednesday plan; parts of it are now superseded (see `02`).

## The user and the story

- The user did an **experimental materials science undergrad**, then joined a **fully simulation-based group for their PhD**, and struggled a lot.
- Groupmates came from **physics, math and CS**. They all struggled, and each forgot to use their own unique strengths.
- That is the motivation. The user's own profile is the natural demo persona: an experimentalist (thin films, XRD, SEM, annealing) learning DFT simulation.

## The core idea (the user's highlight)

> "In the LLM era, the amount of information is not the point. What matters is the **mind flow** to screen and learn effectively, combining your own strengths."

- **Pitch line:** *Most learning tools tell you what you're missing. Explorer also shows you what you bring.*
- **Headline:** **a path, not information.** Explorer designs a personal path: what to focus on, what to skip, which strengths to use.
- **Closing line:** *Explorer: start from where you stand, see what you bring, and enjoy finding out the rest.*

## Ambitions (from the notes)

- **Not only "what you need to learn", but the specific strengths you can apply.** The mind flow of exploring may outweigh the knowledge itself.
- **The fishing-team metaphor:** complementary strengths multiply.
  - Hiring one more fisher gives 5·(n+1): it adds 5.
  - A sashimi processor gives 5·n·1.3: it adds 1.5n, which wins once the team has 4 or more.
  - Research groups differ from companies, but the logic holds for collaboration.
- **Collaborators:** understand what collaborators do and what they might want from you.
- **Interest first:** questions that spark curiosity, not bullet lists, using curiosity lenses (mechanism, origin, limits, optimisation, history, culture, value and career). For example, planes: how do they fly, how high, their history. Magnetism: its origin, its uses, the best-paid jobs that use it.
- **Pop-out quizzes** along the way, as a sanity and attention check.
- **Style:** 3Blue1Brown-like, animated; colour themes Black & Gold or Blue & White.
- **Models:** prefer Haiku for budget. Let people with their own subscription choose their own model (partly done: own API key).

## Two modes, one engine

| | Researcher | Kids |
|---|---|---|
| For | Experts moving into a new field or starting a collaboration | Curious children, about 5–12 |
| Intake | Level → general expertise (multi) + specific expertise → skills & hobbies → topic (+ what you know, why, what sparked it) → papers or notes (≤10 MB locally, 4 MB hosted) | Age → what you love → topic. **No background check** |
| Look | A constellation: Earth = "You", a spaceship flies star to star; Black & Gold or Blue & White | A flower garden or a growing tree, by day or night |
| Map | Your skills lit from the start; core, bridge, strength (gold star) and frontier questions; analogy lines back to your skills; **"Your path"** first | "💡 Imagine…" moments from everyday life; one playful choice per question; blooms |
| Own question | ✦ Ask: a comet lands on the closest star and a new star appears | Watering can (typed or spoken): a new branch grows |
| Goal | Make the most of your strengths | Curiosity and fun; the habit of asking "why?" |

## Why it's different from a chatbot

| Chatbot | Explorer |
|---|---|
| You must know what to ask | It asks you, in a deliberate order |
| A scroll of text that's quickly lost | A persistent map that lights up and grows |
| Generic explanations | Each idea bridged to your background or everyday life |
| Lists what you lack | Also shows what you bring |

## Highlights to show judges

1. **"Your path":** what to focus on, what to skip, which strengths to use. For example, for the user's persona: "your experimental habits of calibrating, controlling variables and comparing to data will help most".
2. **Strengths, not just gaps:** lit skills, gold strength stars, bridges back to your skills.
3. **A journey from the first click:** Earth and a spaceship, or a seed that grows with every intake answer.
4. **One engine, two audiences.**
5. **Your own questions grow the map** (comet, or a watering can you can talk to).
6. **Any topic, live, under a cent per map,** with the key kept private. Researchers can use their own API key.
